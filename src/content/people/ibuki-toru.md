Born Daimaru Chisato, Ibuki Tôru was a staple of Toho's science fiction films in
the 1960s and 70s. With his sharp features and intense gaze, he was frequently
typecast as a villain, henchman, or alien invader.

He is best remembered by _kaiju_ fans for his antagonistic supporting roles,
such as one of Malmess's assassins in _Ghidorah, the Three-Headed Monster_
(1964) and a member of the X-_seijin_ invasion force in _Invasion of
Astro-Monster_ (1965). He also played a rare sympathetic role in _Ebirah, Horror
of the Deep_ (1966) as Yata, the lost brother whose disappearance sparks the
film's adventure.

Following the collapse of Toho's contract system in the early 1970s, he migrated
to television, appearing in various dramas and _tokusatsu_ series. After
retiring from acting, he founded a company specializing in the manufacturing and
sale of hydrogen water, operating under his birth name.
