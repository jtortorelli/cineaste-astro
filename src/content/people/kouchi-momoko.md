Descended from a distinguished lineage, Kouchi Momoko was the granddaughter of
Viscount Ôkouchi Masatoshi, a prominent physicist and politician. She joined
Toho in 1953 as part of their 6th "New Face" class, training alongside future
stars Takarada Akira and Sahara Kenji.

She was catapulted to fame with her role as Yamane Emiko in the original
_Godzilla_ (1954). However, she soon found herself typecast in science fiction,
appearing in the yeti film _Half Human_ (1955) and as the heroine in _The
Mysterians_ (1957). Seeking to broaden her range, she left Toho in 1958 to focus
on stage acting and television, where she enjoyed a long and varied career.

In a poetic bookend to her film career, she returned to the franchise that made
her famous one last time, reprising the role of Emiko in _Godzilla vs.
Destoroyah_ (1995). It was her final film appearance before her death from colon
cancer in 1998.
