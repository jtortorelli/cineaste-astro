Ôki was a versatile actor who worked in film, television, and voice acting. In live-action, he appeared in supporting roles in films like _Yojimbo_ (1961) and _The Adventure of Kigan Castle_ (1966), often playing tough or villainous characters.

He found significant fame as a voice actor, particularly in the _Super Robot Wars_ video game franchise, where he voiced the character Drake Luft. He also provided voices for anime such as _Berserk_ (1997) and _Trigun_ (1998). His deep, commanding voice made him a natural fit for antagonists and authority figures.
