Nakamura Ganjirô II was born into a distinguished Kabuki lineage, the son of Ganjirô I. He inherited his father's name and position, becoming a master of the art form, particularly in the _wagoto_ (romantic lead) style. In 1967, he was designated a Living National Treasure by the Japanese government.

Though primarily a stage actor, Ganjirô II had a significant film career, notably with Daiei Studios. He worked with legendary directors such as Ozu Yasujirô in _Floating Weeds_ (1959), where he played the leader of a traveling theater troupe, and Ichikawa Kon in _Odd Obsession_ (1959) and _Conflagration_ (1958). He also appeared in Kurosawa Akira's _The Lower Depths_ (1957). His son, Sakata Tojurô IV, succeeded him as Ganjirô III.
