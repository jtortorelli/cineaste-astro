Nakakita joined Toho in 1944 after graduating from film school. She appeared in early films by Kurosawa Akira, such as _One Wonderful Sunday_ (1947) and _Drunken Angel_ (1948), and became a regular in the works of director Naruse Mikio, earning a Blue Ribbon Award for her performance in _Lightning_ (1952).

She married legendary Toho producer Tanaka Tomoyuki in 1950. Genre fans recognize her from her roles in _The Last War_ (1961), where she played a mother separated from her family, and _Ebirah, Horror of the Deep_ (1966). In her later years, she became a familiar face on television commercials for Nippon Life Insurance, earning the nickname "Nissei's Auntie."
