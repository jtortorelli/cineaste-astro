Sonoda Ayumi (born Iwadate Yûko) was a Japanese actress active for a brief period during the Golden Age of Toho. 

Sonoda joined Toho Studios in 1955 and made her feature film debut in _Love Never Fails_. During her remarkably short career, she frequently appeared as a supporting player in various salaryman comedies for the studio, including entries in the long-running _Shachô_ ("Company President") film series. 

Her singular, exclusive leading role was as the inquisitive journalist Shinjô Yuriko in Honda Ishirô's giant monster picture _Varan the Unbelievable_ (1958). That same year she also appeared as an exotic nightclub dancer in scandalously revealing attire in another Honda thriller, _The H-Man_. 

She abruptly retired from acting in 1963. After her retirement from the screen, she remained tangentially involved in the entertainment industry, taking up an administrative position in the Min-On Production company.
