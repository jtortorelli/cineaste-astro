Nihei joined Toho's "New Face" program in 1960 and made his screen debut the following year. He quickly became a regular in action and comedy films, often appearing alongside the Crazy Cats. However, he is immortalized in pop culture history for his role as Ide in the original _Ultraman_ (1966). As the Science Patrol's comic relief and weapons expert, Nihei brought warmth and humor to the show, creating a beloved character that defined the archetype for future _Ultra_ series.

Beyond _Ultraman_, Nihei had a robust career in genre film and television. He played Astronaut Itô in _Gorath_ (1962), a role that showcased his dramatic chops, and appeared in _Mighty Jack_ (1968). He remained connected to the _Ultra_ franchise throughout his life, returning for cameos and voice roles in series like _The Ultraman_ (1979) and _Ultraman Max_ (2005). He passed away in 2021 from aspiration pneumonia.
