Rhodes Reason was an American actor and the younger brother of actor Rex Reason (_This Island Earth_). He acted primarily in television, playing guest roles in series such as _Bus Stop_, _Sky King_, _Sugarfoot_, _Frontier Doctor_, _Maverick_, _Perry Mason_, _The Time Tunnel_, and _Star Trek_.

Reason is best known to Japanese film fans as Commander Carl Nelson, the lead scientist studying King Kong in Toho's _King Kong Escapes_ (1967). In a later interview with author Stuart Galbraith IV, Reason playfully commented on the extravagant set design of the film's submarine—including an incongruous vase of flowers—and the novel experience of being made-up by Japanese artists, which involved a surprising amount of eyeliner.

Later in his career, Reason played the role of Daddy Warbucks in the Broadway production of _Annie_, replacing the original actor Reid Shelton for the remainder of its run.
